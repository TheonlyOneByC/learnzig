Zig introduction

Let's first go over with what Zig is. Zig is a low-level programming language(meaning it gives releavly direct control on how your computer executes instructions) Zig first created in 2016 by Andrew Kelley. Zig was created as a direct alternative to C++ designed to have no hidden executions. To simplify what that means imagine it like this You have an plate with 2 apples and you want to add 1 more but there's just not enough room C++ would switch the plate for you but in Zig you have to switch plates manually. However that is not always true many factors contribute to who brings the plate.
